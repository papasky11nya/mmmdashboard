Adstock Decay Model — Carryover Effect

Adstock models how advertising effects persist beyond the period they ran. A decay rate (λ) of 0.0 = no carryover; 0.9 = very long memory. TV and OOH typically have high carryover; digital performance channels have low carryover.

Saturation Curve — Diminishing Returns Modeling

At low spend, each additional rupiah drives strong response. Past the saturation inflection point, marginal returns fall rapidly. The Hill function models this: response = spendα / (Kα + spendα). K = half-saturation spend point, α = shape steepness.
